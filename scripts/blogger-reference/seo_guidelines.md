# SEO Guidelines for GMP Blog Posts

These rules govern every blog post generated for growthmindsetparenting.com. Apply all of them without exception.

---

## Post Length

Target: **1,500–2,500 words.**

- Under 1,500 words: too thin for Google to treat as a meaningful resource
- Over 2,500 words: fine if the content earns it; do not pad to hit a target

---

## Structure

Every post follows this four-part structure in order:

1. **Scene-opener** — Open with a specific, sensory moment. No preamble, no thesis statement. Drop the reader into the scene.
2. **Reframe** — Name what's actually happening developmentally. Flip the parent's assumption.
3. **Research/evidence** — 1–3 sentences backing the reframe with named researchers or frameworks (Yeager, Siegel, Damour are natural fits).
4. **Practical close** — One specific action. No bullet list of tips. One thing, done well.

---

## Title Rules

- The title **must contain the primary keyword phrase**
- Aim for 50–60 characters (Google's display limit is ~60)
- Write for humans first; the keyword fits naturally, not awkwardly
- Avoid clickbait — Sean's audience is intelligent parents who distrust hype

**Examples of good title format:**
- "Why Middle Schoolers Pull Away (And What to Do Instead)"
- "How to Talk to Your Middle Schooler When They Won't Talk Back"
- "The Real Reason Your Kid Shuts Down After School"

---

## Keyword Rules

- The **primary keyword phrase appears in the title and in the first paragraph**
- The keyword appears **3–5 times total** in the post body — no more
- **No keyword stuffing.** If a sentence sounds like it was written for a search engine, rewrite it
- Use natural keyword variations in H2 subheadings (not exact repeats)
- One keyword per post — do not try to rank for two topics at once

---

## Heading Structure

- Use **2–4 H2 subheadings** per post
- H2s should contain keyword variations where natural — not forced repetition
- Use **H3 for sub-points** under an H2 where content genuinely branches (use sparingly)
- No H1 in the post body — the post title is the H1
- Subheadings must be scannable: a reader skimming H2s should understand the arc of the post

**What NOT to do:**
- Do not create more than 4 H2s — this is a narrative post, not a listicle
- Do not write H2s that are just "Section 1," "Section 2," etc.
- Do not use bold text as a substitute for proper heading tags

---

## First Paragraph

- Must contain the **primary keyword phrase**, placed naturally
- Sets up the scene or tension — does not summarize what the post will cover
- No "In this post, I will..." framing
- 3–5 sentences maximum

---

## Internal Links

- Include **1–2 internal links** to related content on the site using the path `/writing/<slug>`
- Links must appear where they are genuinely relevant — never forced
- Anchor text should be descriptive (not "click here" or "read more")
- If no closely related post exists yet, omit rather than link irrelevantly

---

## Meta Description

- Length: **150–160 characters** (count carefully — Google truncates at 160)
- Must include the **primary keyword phrase**
- Must be a **complete sentence** — not a fragment or a list
- Written for humans, not for robots — a real sentence that makes someone want to read the post
- Does not start with the site name or author name

**Example format:**
"When your middle schooler stops talking to you, it's not rejection — it's development. Here's what the research says about why it happens and what actually helps."

---

## What NOT to Do

- **No keyword stuffing** — if the keyword appears in every paragraph, rewrite
- **No duplicate H2s** — each subheading covers distinct ground
- **No listicle endings** — "5 things to try tonight" is not Sean's voice and hurts dwell time
- **No thin content** — every paragraph must earn its place
- **No wall-of-text sections** — paragraphs in the post body should be 3–5 sentences max; break up long sections with H2s or white space
- **No generic meta descriptions** — "Read this post to learn about middle schoolers" is not acceptable

---

## Checklist Before Submitting a Post

- [ ] Title contains primary keyword phrase
- [ ] First paragraph contains primary keyword phrase
- [ ] Keyword appears 3–5 times total in body (count it)
- [ ] 2–4 H2 subheadings present; H2s contain keyword variations where natural
- [ ] Post is 1,500–2,500 words
- [ ] 1–2 internal links to `/writing/<slug>` where relevant
- [ ] Meta description is 150–160 characters, includes keyword, is a complete sentence
- [ ] Structure follows: Scene-opener → Reframe → Research → Practical close
- [ ] Post ends with one action, not a list
